# Intelligent Chest Hospital System

## Overview

The Intelligent Chest Hospital System is a comprehensive solution designed to streamline patient data management and medical examination processes within chest hospitals. This system digitizes the handling of patient information, medical tests, and reports, improving efficiency and accuracy.

## My Role

In this project, I was responsible for the following key areas:

*   **System Analysis**: Conducted in-depth analysis of the hospital's existing workflows and data management practices. This involved face-to-face surveys with doctors, the hospital director, and other staff members at Damanhour Chest Hospital to understand their specific needs and challenges.
*   **Database Design and Management**: Developed and managed the project's central database. This included designing the database schema, ensuring data integrity, and writing complex queries for data analysis and retrieval.
*   **Data Analysis**: Performed detailed analysis of the data to extract meaningful insights and support the system's functionality.
*   **Research Collaboration**: Participated in a research team that utilized AI for CT scan analysis to assist in chest-related diagnoses.

## Database Design

The database was designed to capture all relevant information, including:

*   Patient demographics and medical history
*   Medical examination results (e.g., CT scans, X-rays)
*   Diagnostic reports
*   Treatment plans

## System Analysis and Requirements Gathering

To ensure the system met the hospital's needs, I conducted face-to-face surveys with doctors, the hospital director, and staff. This process helped in:

*   Understanding the current data management challenges
*   Identifying key requirements for the new system
*   Gathering feedback on the proposed design

## AI-Powered CT Scan Analysis

As part of the research team, I contributed to the development of an AI-driven solution for analyzing CT scans. This component aimed to:

*   Improve the accuracy and speed of diagnoses
*   Assist medical professionals in identifying potential issues
